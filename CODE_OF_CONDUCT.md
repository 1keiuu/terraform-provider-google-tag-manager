# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Project participants are expected to provide a welcoming, safe, and respectful
environment for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or
ability.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate constructively and respectfully.
- Welcome questions and differing technical perspectives.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Protect private information and credentials.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate intimidation, and publication
of private information are not tolerated.

Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when behavior
is inconsistent with this policy. Reports can be submitted privately through
the repository's security contact channel.
